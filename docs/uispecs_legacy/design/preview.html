Contrast — the headline finding

PRD.md §16 requires WCAG compliance. 7 of 24 measured pairs failed AA, and the worst was also the most-used colour in the interface:

#9a968f at 2.80:1, 49 occurrences, carrying every table header, mono label and timestamp → now #716d67 at 4.52:1 worst case.

Solving for the accessible floor showed the palette cannot support three distinct quiet-grey text tiers. The fix keeps two and documents the collapse.

Originals preserved as *.orig. Regenerate everything with the four scripts in design/scripts/ — standard library only, no dependencies, idempotent across 73 files.
